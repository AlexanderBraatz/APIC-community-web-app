      This Privacy Policy explains how **IMMOBILIARI DI CASTELFALFI ETS**
      (“APIC”, “we”, “us”) processes personal data when you use the APIC Owners
      Community website. Registered details: Largo Don Pino Puglisi, 6, 56028 San
      Miniato PI; Tax Code (Codice Fiscale) 90073880503; National Register of the
      Third Sector (RUNTS) Registration No. 146236.


      The members area is invitation-only and is intended for Castelfalfi
      property owners and authorised community members. Public pages (such as the
      home page, About, and blog) are not used for usage analytics or product
      insights.


      **Purposes.** APIC ETS processes personal data of its members solely for
      membership administration, communication, and the operation of this
      members-only area — including invitations, authentication, the Attendance
      Calendar, directory and place tools, and related community features.


      **What we process.** We process account and membership information such as
      your name, email address, profile details (for example a profile colour),
      role (member or admin), and invitation or account status. If you use
      community features, we also process information you choose to share with
      other members — for example attendance or stay dates and optional notes on
      the Attendance Calendar, directory-related details, and content you interact
      with in the members area. We keep operational records needed for
      invitations, authentication, and administration (including an admin audit
      log of certain administrative actions). Please do not upload sensitive
      personal data beyond what is needed for community coordination.


      **Hosting and core processors.** The website is hosted on our
      infrastructure providers. Authentication and member database services are
      provided by **Supabase**. Content for public and editorial pages may be
      managed with **TinaCMS** / TinaCloud. These processors act on our behalf so
      we can deliver accounts, the members area, and site content.


      **Optional usage analytics and product insights.** With your consent, we use
      **PostHog** (Cloud EU) for two optional, independent settings. Both default
      to off until you choose. There is no public visitor tracking and no cookie
      banner for anonymous browsing.


      **Usage analytics** helps us understand which pages and features are most
      useful. When enabled, we may collect events such as page views and feature
      use (for example opening the calendar or directory filters). Event
      properties are limited to non-identifying details where possible. We
      identify analytics profiles with your account UUID only — not your email,
      name, or phone number.


      **Product insights** provides optional insights into how you use the site
      so we can improve it. When enabled, this may include signed-in page visits
      (including community calendar and member content) so we can find friction
      and fix problems. Password and similar credential fields are masked. Auth
      credential routes (sign-in, accept invite, forgot/reset password) are
      excluded.


      Community administrators may be invited to the PostHog project externally to
      review aggregate insights. There is no in-app analytics dashboard.
      Declining or turning off these options does not reduce your access to
      community features.


      **Essential error monitoring.** To keep the service reliable we use
      **Sentry** for essential technical error monitoring. This runs for signed-in
      use of the application independently of PostHog consent: declining optional
      analytics does not turn off error monitoring. Sentry is configured to
      minimise personal data (for example we do not send default PII, and we scrub
      cookies, auth headers, tokens, passwords, and similar sensitive fields from
      error reports where practicable). Sentry is used by developers for
      troubleshooting; production alerts may be routed to an internal operations
      channel without member contact details.


      **Consent and your choices.** When you accept an invitation you must agree
      to the Terms & Conditions. Separately, you are asked to choose optional
      usage analytics and product insights. Agreeing to the Terms is required to
      join; these optional choices are not. After joining, you can change usage
      analytics and product insights at any time under **Account → Privacy**.
      Turning a preference off stops further capture as soon as the preference is
      applied. Essential error monitoring remains on.
      Returning members are not shown a soft consent banner; preferences are
      managed from Account → Privacy.


      **Data minimisation and retention.** We aim to process only what is needed
      to run the community, invitations, and the features you use. Analytics
      events are designed to avoid unnecessary personal content (for example
      search text is not sent as a raw query). Account and membership data are
      retained while your membership is active and as needed for administration,
      security, and legal obligations. Optional usage analytics and product
      insights data are processed according to your preferences and the retention
      settings of our PostHog EU project. Error reports are retained according to
      our Sentry project settings for diagnosing and fixing issues.


      **Cookies and similar technologies.** This website uses technically
      necessary cookies (and similar storage) required for login and core site
      functionality. Optional usage analytics and product insights run only if
      you consent; when enabled, PostHog may use cookies or local storage for
      those purposes. Declining them means those optional technologies are not
      used for you.


      **Sharing and access.** Membership information you share in community
      features (such as attendance dates) is visible to other authorised members
      as part of how those features work. Administrative staff and systems
      administrators may access data as needed to operate and secure the service.
      We do not sell your personal data. Processors listed above receive data only
      to provide their services to us. Personal data is only disclosed externally
      where required by law or necessary for association operations.


      **Your rights and contact.** Depending on applicable law (including EU/EEA
      data protection rules), you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, or
      restrict certain processing, to object to certain processing, and to data
      portability. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For
      inquiries regarding your personal data, contact us at
      **associazioneproprietaricastelfalfi@pec.it**, or use the association
      details in the site footer. You can update many profile and privacy settings
      yourself in Account.


      We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The version published
      on this page is the current one.
