# Holiday community platform — features included

A private website for a holiday-home owners’ community. Members see who else will be on site, and they browse a curated directory of local places. Administrators run membership, the directory, and the news pages themselves.

**Out of the box:** every feature in this document.  
**Customised for your brand:** logo, colours, photography, video, wording, page layout, the map centre, and the emails members receive. The product stays the same. The look becomes yours.

The interface is in English. Member and admin handbooks are also available in German and Italian.

---

## 1. Public website

Anyone can open the site without an account.

- **Home page** with a full-width hero (background video, or a still image), a feature grid, and a closing banner with a contact button.
- **Four local category pages:** Food & Dining, Services & Maintenance, Health & Wellness, and Shop & Market. Each has its own hero and introduction. The full directory and map sit on these pages and open after sign-in, with a clear “sign in to browse” prompt for visitors.
- **Legal pages:** Legal information, Privacy Policy, and Terms & Conditions, linked from the footer.
- **Header navigation** across the main sections, with the current page marked.
- **Sign in** from the header. There is no public registration link.
- **Footer** with logo, legal links, and a quiet link to the content editor.
- **A proper “page not found” screen** when someone follows a bad link.

About, the members hub, the attendance calendar, and News, Events & Activities are for signed-in members.

## 2. Who can see what

Two roles: **member** and **admin**.

| | Visitor | Member | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing pages and legal pages | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Local directory and map | Prompted to sign in | Yes | Yes |
| Attendance calendar | No | Yes | Yes |
| News, events, and About | No | Yes | Yes |
| Own profile and password | No | Yes | Yes |
| Invite people, change roles, edit the directory and articles | No | No | Yes |

- New people join only by invitation. Public sign-up is switched off.
- A member who is still finishing setup is kept on the join steps until they are done.
- After sign-in, people land on the members hub, or back on the page they were trying to open.
- Members see each other’s display name and colour. They do not see each other’s email addresses. Admins can see emails.
- Admins never see or set another person’s password.

## 3. Joining and accounts

### Invitation

- An admin invites by email. The person receives a branded **“you’re invited”** email with a setup link.
- The link is valid for one hour. If it expires, the person requests an **8-digit one-time code** by email and continues from there.
- Admins can invite **one address or a whole list at once**. Addresses that are already members, or already invited, are skipped and reported.
- Pending invitations can be **resent** or **cancelled**. A history of accepted and cancelled invitations is kept.
- Someone who starts joining and loses the email can still be resent a link, because they stay on the allowlist until they finish.

### Setup steps

The new member walks through a short wizard:

1. **Password** (at least 8 characters, entered twice).
2. **Privacy.** They must accept the terms and confirm they have read the privacy policy. Usage analytics and product insights are optional and can be turned off.
3. **Shown name** — the name other members see. It starts empty, so they choose it themselves.
4. **Profile colour** — used behind their initial and on their stays in the calendar. Ten colours.
5. **First stays** — optional. They can add arrival and departure dates before they even enter the site, or skip.
6. **Favourite members** — optional. They can pin people they want at the top of the calendar, or skip.
7. **Handbook language** — English, German, or Italian.

A short **welcome screen** then explains the map, the calendar, and where to find help, in the language they chose.

### Everyday account

From the account page a member can:

- See their name, email, role, and colour.
- Change their shown name.
- Change their profile colour.
- Change their handbook language.
- Update analytics choices.
- Change their password.
- Open the member handbook, or download it as a PDF.
- Sign out.
- Return to the members hub.

### Forgotten password

- Request a one-time code by email.
- Enter the code and set a new password.
- Request a fresh code if the old one has expired.
- The reset email is branded to match the invitation.

## 4. Members hub

A private home page after sign-in.

- Hero and a short welcome.
- **Category grid** into the four local directories, each with an image, a description, and a button.
- A section that leads into the attendance calendar (“who is here”).
- Editable in the visual content editor, like the public pages.

## 5. Attendance calendar — who is here

A shared timeline of stays. This is presence (“I will be there from Friday to Monday”), not a booking system.

- **One row per member**, with their name and colour.
- **Stays drawn as bars** across the days, in that member’s colour, showing the title and a short preview of the note.
- The timeline runs from **this week and about two years ahead**, day by day, starting on Monday. People scroll sideways through the season.
- **Current and upcoming stays** are shown. Stays that have already ended drop off the calendar.
- A member can have **overlapping stays** (for example a long summer and a shorter trip inside it).

### Managing your own stays

- **Manage attendance** opens a list of your stays.
- Add a stay: start date, end date, title, and an optional note. New stays cannot start before today.
- Edit any of your stays. Delete one, with a confirmation.
- Changes save immediately.

### Comparing people

- Search members by name and **pin** the ones you care about. Pinned people stay at the top of the calendar.
- Pins are saved on the account, so they are still there next time.
- The same pins can be chosen during joining.
- Click another member’s stay to open a **read-only** view of their dates, title, and note. You cannot change their data.

### Calendar comfort

- **Text size:** small, medium, or large. The choice is remembered.
- **Your colour** can also be changed from the calendar settings, and other members see it on your row and your stays.
- **On a phone:** show or hide the name column, and drag the divider to make the names wider or narrower, so the days get more room.

### What admins can do on the calendar

- Create, edit, and delete **any member’s** stays.
- Search for the member inside Manage attendance and switch to their list.

## 6. Local recommendations

A curated directory of places the community actually trusts: restaurants, trades, doctors, shops, and so on. Members browse it. Admins maintain it. Members do not publish their own listings.

### Browsing

- Browse inside a category, or search across places.
- **Search** by business name, type of place, or keyword. Matching is forgiving of typos, and it also matches hidden synonyms (for example an Italian word for the same thing).
- **Keyword chips** narrow the list. Chips can be removed one by one.
- A selected place can be focused on its own.
- Results update in the page address, so a useful search can be shared as a link.
- Empty searches say so plainly.

### Each place shows

- Name and type (for example “Osteria” or “Plumber”).
- The community’s own description.
- Address.
- Opening hours, including a free-text note such as “by appointment only”.
- Contacts, each with an optional label such as “Owner” or “Reservations”:
  - Phone (tap to call)
  - Mobile
  - WhatsApp
  - Email
  - Website
- **Open in Google Maps.**
- **Show on the community map.**

### Map

- Google map centred on the community.
- A pin for every recommended place.
- Select a pin to focus that place, and jump back to the full overview.
- Works on desktop and on a phone.

## 7. News, events, and activities

A private magazine for the community: dinners, works, trips, notices.

### Reading

- An index of articles with cover image, title, short introduction, author, and date.
- **Search** by title or introduction.
- The article page: cover image, author, date, optional map of the place the article is about, and the body.
- **Images in the article open in a viewer** you can step through.
- The article’s place can be opened in Google Maps.
- If an article’s web address changes, the **old address still works** and forwards to the new one.

### Writing (admins)

A guided form:

1. Title, author, publish date, and short introduction.
2. Cover image: upload a new one, or pick from covers already used. Images are compressed before upload. JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF.
3. Location: optional. Search Google for the place, confirm the pin, or skip.
4. Body, with a formatting bar: paragraph, two heading levels, bold, italic, links, bullet lists, numbered lists, and images in the text.
5. Preview, then publish.

- **Drafts save automatically** while you write.
- A draft stays private. Admins see a draft badge and can still open it.
- Publishing asks for a cover image and some body text before it will go live.
- A live article can be **turned back into a draft**, edited, or deleted.
- From the article list, admins can add, edit, or delete without leaving the page.

## 8. Help, handbooks, and welcome

- A **help button** on the site. It opens the handbook on the section that matches the page you are on (the map, the calendar, invitations, and so on). Inside the visual editor it opens the website-content chapter.
- A small **coach mark** points the help button out on first visits. It can be dismissed, and it can be turned off for good.
- **Report a problem** from the same button. The report goes to the people who look after the site.
- **Welcome screen** after joining, in English, German, or Italian.
- **Member handbook** and **admin handbook**, on screen and as a downloadable PDF.
- Handbook language follows the account setting: **English, German, or Italian**. The rest of the site stays in English.

## 9. Administration

Admins have their own area, separate from the visual content editor.

### Dashboard

- Counts for members, articles, recommendations, and calendar entries.
- Shortcuts: add a member, add a recommendation, add an article.
- Links to both handbooks and their PDFs.
- Previews of the three emails the site sends: invitation, join code, and password reset.

### Members

- A searchable list of everyone, with name, email, and role.
- **Promote** a member to admin, and **demote** an admin back to member, each with a confirmation.
- **Remove** a member, with a confirmation.
- The **last remaining admin cannot be demoted or deleted**, and an admin cannot remove their own account if nobody else would be left to run the community.

### Recommendations — adding a place

A step-by-step form, so a complete listing is easy to get right:

1. Choose the category.
2. Search Google for the business, biased toward the area around the community, and pick the right one.
3. Confirm the location on the map.
4. Review what was imported: name, address, map coordinates, phone, website, opening hours, and the kind of place.
5. Adjust the type and the name.
6. Adjust the pin if it needs a nudge.
7. Add extra contacts (phone, mobile, WhatsApp, email, website) with labels.
8. Adjust opening hours and add a note.
9. Write the community’s own description.
10. **Keyword suggestions are drafted automatically** from the place, then you accept, remove, search existing keywords, or create new ones. New keywords can also receive hidden search synonyms (other languages, common misspellings).
11. Review every step, jump back to fix one, and create the recommendation.

Existing recommendations can be edited through the same form, searched in the admin list, and deleted with a confirmation.

### Keywords

- A shared vocabulary used by search.
- Each keyword has a public name and **hidden aliases** (synonyms and other-language names) so search still finds the place.
- Rename a keyword, edit its aliases, or delete it. Deleting removes it from every recommendation that used it.
- A **coverage view** shows which recommendations have few or no keywords, and lets you jump to the ones that share a keyword.
- Missing aliases can be filled in automatically.

### Website content

A visual editor for the marketing pages and the members hub. No code.

- Click a text field on the page and edit it. The page updates as you type.
- Replace images, upload new ones, and add captions.
- **Add blocks, drag them into a new order, or delete them.**
- Save to publish.

Blocks available:

- Hero banner (heading, subtitle, background video, background image).
- Feature grid (icon, label, description, image).
- Quote or statement banner with a button.
- Button list.
- Category grid (image, title, description, link, button label).
- Call-to-action section.
- Section heading.
- Text.
- Image and caption list.
- The recommendations map and search block, with its own heading and caption.

### Activity log

A record of sensitive admin actions: invitations sent, resent, and cancelled; promotions and demotions; members removed; recommendations created, updated, deleted, and placed on the map; keywords created, updated, deleted, and alias updates.

- Each entry shows who did it, when, and a short summary.
- Open an entry to see the previous values and the new values.
- Filter by the kind of action, the kind of record, or a search.

## 10. Privacy, reliability, and devices

- Terms must be accepted before someone enters the community.
- **Usage analytics** and **product insights** (including session replay) are optional and can be changed later from the account page.
- Analytics is kept off the password and one-time-code screens.
- Essential error monitoring stays on so breakages can be found and fixed. That part is not optional.
- Useful product events can be measured when a member has opted in: sign-in, invite accepted, calendar viewed, stay created or edited, directory searched or filtered, a place opened, the map opened, a contact tapped, an article opened, a gallery opened.
- The site is built for **phones and desktops**: the calendar, the map, the directory, the articles, and the admin forms all have layouts for a small screen.
- Member data lives in a hosted database with access rules enforced on the server, so the browser cannot simply ask for another member’s email or another admin’s tools.

## Outside this package

These are deliberate boundaries, so expectations stay clear:

- People cannot register themselves. An admin invites them.
- There is no in-app chat or messaging.
- There is no booking of houses, no payments, and no rental inventory. The calendar records who will be there.
- Members do not add their own recommendations. Admins curate the directory.
- The calendar is for current and upcoming stays. It is not an archive of past seasons.
- The day-to-day interface is English. The handbooks are English, German, and Italian.
