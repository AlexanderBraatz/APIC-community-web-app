## Recommendations {#recommendations}

### Overview {#overview}

Add or edit places via the recommendation stepper-form, or remove recommendations.

### How to Add a Recommendation {#how-to-add-a-recommendation}

1. If you are unsure of the name of the business, search for it on Google Maps.
2. Find the business you want to recommend.
3. Copy the exact name as listed on Google Maps, as the website uses Google data to fill out the rest of the information.

![Find business name on Google Maps](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-1.jpg)

1. Click [**Add Recommendation**](/members/admin/listings/new).

![Add Recommendation](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-2.jpg)

1. Select a category.
2. Click **Next**.

![Select category](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-3.jpg)

1. Search for the business by name. (The name it has on Google Maps)
2. Click to select the right business.

![Search and select business](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-4.jpg)

1. Check that the location looks correct.
2. Click **Next** to continue.

![Confirm location](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-5.jpg)

1. Wait while the website gets the other information from Google Maps.
2. Click **Next** to continue.

![Wait for Google data](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-6.jpg)

1. Add extra contact information.

![Add extra contact](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-7.jpg)

1. Change the type of contact info (e.g. email or WhatsApp).
2. Add an extra label (e.g. Owner or reservations).
3. Add the information.
4. Click **Next** to continue.

![Fill contact details](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-8.jpg)

1. Add or change opening hours.
2. Add a note (e.g. by appointment only).
3. Click **Next** to continue.

![Opening hours and note](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-9.jpg)

1. Add your personal touch by adding a description.
2. Click **Next** to continue.

![Add description](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-10.jpg)

1. Wait while the website gets keyword suggestions from OpenAI.
2. Click **Next** to continue.

![Wait for keywords](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-11.jpg)

1. Check the suggested keywords.

![Suggested keywords](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-12.jpg)

1. Type to search for existing keywords.
2. Click on keywords you would like to add to the recommendation.

![Search existing keywords](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-13.jpg)

1. Type to spell out a new keyword.
2. Click on the new keywords shown as a green box under the search bar.

![Create new keyword](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-14.jpg)

1. Check the list of keywords to see the ones you have added.
2. Click **Next** to continue.

![Review keywords](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-15.jpg)

1. Double-check all the information.
2. Click on any particular step to go back and change the information.
3. Click **Create recommendation** to finish the process.

![Create recommendation](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-recommendation-16.jpg)

### How to Delete a Recommendation {#how-to-delete-a-recommendation}

1. Click [**Recommendations**](/members/admin/listings).

![Open Recommendations](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-recommendation-1.jpg)

2. Scroll down.

![Scroll recommendations](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-recommendation-2.jpg)

3. Search for the recommendation.

![Search recommendation](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-recommendation-3.jpg)

4. Click the **X**.

![Click X](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-recommendation-4.jpg)

5. Click **Delete**.

![Confirm delete](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-recommendation-5.jpg)

## Members {#members}

### Overview {#overview}

Invite new members by email, promote members to admin, or remove members.

### How to Add a New Member {#how-to-add-a-new-member}

1. Click [**Add Member**](/members/admin/invitations).

![Add Member](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-new-member-1.jpg)

2. Enter an email, or enter a list of emails all at once.
3. Click **Send Invitations**.

![Send invitations](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-new-member-2.jpg)

### How to Promote a Member to Admin {#how-to-promote-a-member-to-admin}

1. Click [**Members**](/members/admin/users).

![Open Members](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-promote-a-member-to-admin-1.jpg)

2. Search for a member.

![Search member](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-promote-a-member-to-admin-2.jpg)

3. Click **Promote**.

![Click Promote](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-promote-a-member-to-admin-3.jpg)

4. Click **Promote**.

![Confirm Promote](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-promote-a-member-to-admin-4.jpg)

### How to Delete a Member {#how-to-delete-a-member}

1. Click [**Members**](/members/admin/users).

![Open Members](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-member-1.jpg)

2. Search for a member.

![Search member](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-member-2.jpg)

3. Click the **X**.

![Click X](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-member-3.jpg)

4. Click **Delete**.

![Confirm Delete](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-member-4.jpg)

5. Observe that the member has been removed.

![Member removed](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-member-5.jpg)

## News, Events & Activities {#posts}

### Overview {#overview}

Create News, Events & Activities posts with the article form.

### How to Add a New News, Events & Activities Post {#how-to-add-a-new-blog-post}

1. Click [**Add article**](/members/admin/blog/new).

![Add new News, Events & Activities article](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-new-blog-post-1.jpg)

1. Add a title and a description / introduction.
2. Click **Next**.

![Add title and description](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-1.jpg)

1. Upload a new cover image.
2. Or select one of the existing cover images.

![Upload or select cover image](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-2.jpg)

1. Click **Next**.

![Click Next after cover image](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-3.jpg)

1. Click **Add location**.

![Click Add location](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-4.jpg)

1. Search for a business or location.

![Search for a business or location](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-5.jpg)

1. Select the business or location.

![Select the business or location](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-6.jpg)

1. Verify the location and click **Next**.

![Verify location and click Next](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-7.jpg)

1. Use the text controls to style the text in the body section of the article.

![Style text in the article body](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-8.jpg)

1. Click **Add image** to add an image to the body of the article.

![Click Add image](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-9.jpg)

1. Select the image you want to add from your files.

![Select image from files](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-10.jpg)

1. Click **Open** to upload that image.

![Click Open to upload image](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-11.jpg)

1. Click **Next** to continue.

![Click Next to continue](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-12.jpg)

1. Add further text under the image.
2. Click **Next** when you are finished editing the article body.

![Add further text and click Next](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-13.jpg)

1. Click **Publish** to publish the article.

![Click Publish](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-new-artikle-image-14.jpg)

## Website Content {#website-content}

### Overview {#overview}

Use Content Admin to edit text, images, and blocks on website pages.

### How to Change an Image on Website Content {#how-to-change-an-image-on-a-blog-post}

1. Click [**Content Admin**](/admin#/~/place).

![Content Admin](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-edit-the-websites-text-and-images-1.jpg)

2. Click on the image you would like to change.

![Select image on page](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-change-an-image-on-a-blog-post-1.jpg)

3. Click on the image in the TinaCMS editor.

![Select image in editor](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-change-an-image-on-a-blog-post-2.jpg)

4. Click **Upload**.

![Click Upload](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-change-an-image-on-a-blog-post-3.jpg)

5. Select the image you would like to upload. (This step may look different on your device.)

![Select file to upload](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-change-an-image-on-a-blog-post-4.jpg)

6. Click **Open**.

![Click Open](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-change-an-image-on-a-blog-post-5.jpg)

7. Click **Insert**.

![Click Insert](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-change-an-image-on-a-blog-post-6.jpg)

8. Add a caption.
9. Save your changes.

![Caption and save](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-change-an-image-on-a-blog-post-7.jpg)

### How to Edit Text on Website Content {#how-to-edit-text-on-a-blog-post}

1. Click [**Content Admin**](/admin#/~/place).

![Content Admin](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-edit-the-websites-text-and-images-1.jpg)

2. Click on any field surrounded by a blue dashed border.

![Click dashed field](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-edit-text-on-a-blog-post-1.jpg)

3. This will open the connected input field in the form on the left.

![Connected input field](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-edit-text-on-a-blog-post-2.jpg)

4. Change the text in the input field and watch the text displayed on the website change at the same time.

![Live text update](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-edit-text-on-a-blog-post-3.jpg)

5. Save your changes to publish them onto the website.

### How to Add a New Block {#how-to-add-a-new-block}

1. In Content Admin, open the page overview for the page you want to edit.

![Open page overview](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-new-block-1.jpg)

2. Click the plus icon to open the blocks selection menu.

![Open blocks menu](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-new-block-2.jpg)

3. Select your choice of block (e.g. Text).

![Select a block](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-new-block-3.jpg)

4. Scroll down on the website to see the new block.

![New block on page](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-new-block-4.jpg)

5. Update the text or other content of the block.
6. Save your changes.

![Update block and save](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-add-a-new-block-5.jpg)

### How to Move Blocks Around {#how-to-move-blocks-around}

1. Click and hold the three dots to start dragging the block.

![Start dragging block](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-move-blocks-around-1.jpg)

2. Drag the block to the position you want.

![Drag block](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-move-blocks-around-2.jpg)

3. Release the mouse and see that the block has moved in the TinaCMS sidebar and on the website.
4. Save your changes.

![Block moved](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-move-blocks-around-3.jpg)

### How to Delete a Block {#how-to-delete-a-blog-post-block}

1. In Content Admin, open the page overview for the page you want to edit.

![Open page overview](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-blog-post-block-1.jpg)

2. Click the bin icon next to the block you wish to delete.

![Click bin icon](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-blog-post-block-2.jpg)

3. Observe that the block has been removed from both the TinaCMS editor and the website preview.
4. Save your changes.

![Block removed](screenshots/screenshotsreal/how-to-delete-a-blog-post-block-3.png)
